From Rented Cameras to Camera X

Hi, I'm Ajay Pawar, but my friends and clients know me as Ajju.

My journey into the world of photography didn't start with a high-end studio. It started in my childhood, fueled by pure passion and a rented camera. I used to save up just to rent a camera for a few hours, running around trying to capture everything I could see. Those days taught me the real value of a single frame.

Today, as a 23-year-old professional photographer based in the bustling heart of Mumbai, my vision has evolved, but that same hunger remains. I built Camera X to deliver more than just photos—I deliver a cinematic, rich-look experience. Whether I'm shooting high-fashion portraits, royal Indian weddings, or raw street vlogs, I aim for perfection.

My Trusted Gear

I currently shoot on my absolute favorite beast: the M5, paired with a razor-sharp 35mm Sigma lens. This combination allows me to capture the depth, the bokeh, and the exact "rich look" that defines my brand's aesthetic.
