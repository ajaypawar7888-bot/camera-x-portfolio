The Origin

From Rented Cameras to a Legacy of Art.

I am Ajay Pawar (Ajju). A 23-year-old visionary from Mumbai, driven by an obsession with light, shadows, and storytelling.

My journey didn't start in luxury; it started with a rented camera and a relentless hustle. Today, operating under Camera X, I deliver a signature "rich look" across fashion ramps, traditional Indian weddings, and daily cinematic vlogs.

  • Master of the M5 & 35mm focal length.
  • Authentic storytelling for brands and couples.
  • Obsessed with the Mumbai street hustle.
