The Vision Behind The Lens

Hello, I'm Ajay Pawar. What started with a rented camera has evolved into a lifelong pursuit of visual perfection. Operating from the heart of Mumbai, my goal is to blend raw, authentic emotions with a premium, high-fashion aesthetic.

Equipped with my trusted M5 and the 35mm Sigma lens, I focus on capturing details that others miss—whether it's the vibrant chaos of a street, the elegance of a summer fashion ramp, or the rich traditions of an Indian celebration.
